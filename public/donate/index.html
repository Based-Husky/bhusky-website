How it works

  1. Your donation goes to the BHUSKY charity wallet. Every transaction is public on BaseScan.
  2. When the bowl reaches $200, we donate the full amount to a dog rescue through Endaoment, rotating between:
    NorthStar Pet Rescue · The REAL Bark · Compassion Without Borders · S.T.A.R.T. Rescue · ROLDA
  3. Every donation is announced on X and Telegram, with its transaction.

The charity wallet is managed by the BHUSKY team. Donations to it are not tax-deductible.
