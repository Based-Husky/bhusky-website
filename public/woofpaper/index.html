1. What is Based Husky?

Based Husky ($BHUSKY) is a community token on Base with one mission: helping rescue dogs. Its mascot, Bhusky, is the one and only blue husky on Base.

$BHUSKY is a community token, not an investment product. Holding it means being part of a community that funds dog rescues and can check where every dollar goes.

2. Our story so far

  • Sep 2024$BHUSKY launches on Base with a fair launch on Apestore: no team, VC or advisor allocation.
  • Dec 2024The community embraces a charity mission. A week later, the first donation goes to Lovable Paws Rescue (South Carolina, USA).
  • Jul–Aug 2025Two donations to Dharamsala Animal Rescue (India), the second one together with Pawthereum and Base Printer for International Dog Day.
  • Sep 2025A custom orthopaedic boot for Pifa, a dog cared for by K9 Rescue (Eastern Europe & Ukraine): our first donation made entirely on Base.
  • Oct 2025The "Adopt with $BHUSKY" NFT cards on vibe.market share their fees with a dog rescue.
  • 2026Bhusky Games: BHUSKY Runner and Bhusky's Crypt open on Telegram, with Bhusky's Shelter coming next.
  • Nov 2026An official charity wallet on Base, the Feed the Bowl donation page and the Impact page with every donation and its proof.

The charity wallet

Since November 2026, donations go through one public wallet on Base: 0xBD425647480C10b476822318979785C800Aefe10 Verify ↗

  1. Anyone can feed the bowl: USDC on Base with Base Pay at bhusky.net/donate, or from any wallet.
  2. Every time it reaches $200, we donate it to a dog rescue through Endaoment (which keeps a small processing fee), rotating between NorthStar Pet Rescue, The REAL Bark, Compassion Without Borders, S.T.A.R.T. Rescue and ROLDA.
  3. Every donation is published on the Impact page with its transaction, like all the ones before it.

Where the money comes from

There is no tax on trades, so buying or selling $BHUSKY does not send money to the charity wallet. Donations come from the community feeding the bowl, from our products and from the BHUSKY team.

The charity wallet is managed by the BHUSKY team. Donations to it are not tax-deductible.

4. Huskenomics

  • Fixed supply of 1,000,000,000 $BHUSKY, with no mint function. Verify ↗
  • 0/0 tax: no fees on buys or sells. Verify ↗
  • Ownership renounced: no one, including us, can change the contract. Verify ↗
  • The original BHUSKY/WETH liquidity on Uniswap V2 is locked forever: its LP tokens are burned. Verify ↗
  • About 14.6% of the supply is permanently out of circulation: about 45.69M burned to the dead address, plus 100.08M that a holder accidentally sent to the token contract on March 9, 2025, where they can never move again. Verify burn ↗ Verify transfer ↗
  • Fair launch: no team, VC or advisor allocation, no vesting and no unlocks. The project's current maintainer holds about 8% of the supply, bought on the open market.

5. What we build

Bhusky Games

BHUSKY Runner Beta

An endless runner on Telegram: jump, dodge, grab coins, stack power-ups and challenge your friends.

Bhusky's Crypt Beta

A roguelike dungeon crawler on Telegram, with two classes, five bosses, permanent upgrades and a Daily Run.

Bhusky's Shelter Coming soon

Tap, build and adopt: grow a dog shelter into a sanctuary, on Telegram and on the web, where players can also feed the bowl.

Today, coins and points inside the games are just for fun: they are not tokens and have no monetary value. Web3 features, including wallet connection, are planned for the final versions of the games, with web versions on Base.

ShelterSite In development

A free, open-source tool by BHUSKY that lets shelters publish multilingual adoption websites for their dogs.

Adopt with $BHUSKY

A collection of NFT cards on vibe.market, part of whose fees go to a dog rescue.

Dog-care tips

Practical tips on dog health and care, shared on X and Telegram with #BhuskyTips, plus dog trivia inside Bhusky's Shelter.

6. Community
